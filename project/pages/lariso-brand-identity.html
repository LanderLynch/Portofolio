02 · Project Overview

Bringing a 1996 Indonesian Snack Brand Online

The website translates Lariso's physical product identity—tempe asli, sagu alami, and long-lasting crispness—into a structured digital brand experience.

Brand Identity & Presence

Communicating Authentic Product Value

Lariso positions its chips around real fermented tempeh and natural sago rather than flour-heavy substitutes. The website establishes a cohesive digital presence through heritage storytelling ("Sejak 1996"), warm packaging visuals, and clear nutritional messaging.

Discovery & Purchase Intent

Structured Promotional Architecture

Designed as a promotional brand website prototype, every section guides visitors from initial product discovery through flavor exploration, comparison against ordinary chips, social proof, and purchase-ready payment indicators (DANA, GoPay, ShopeePay, OVO, BCA).

03 · Business & Marketing Objectives

Four Core Objectives Behind the Website

Objective 01

Brand Awareness

Establish Lariso's heritage ("Sejak 1996") and distinctive Keripik Tempe Sagu identity immediately above the fold.

Objective 02

Product Differentiation

Contrast Lariso against generic chips using ingredient transparency (tempe asli, sagu alami, tanpa pengawet/MSG) and a 7-point comparison table.

Objective 03

Customer Engagement

Encourage exploration through an interactive flavor slider across all 5 variants and relatable customer review cards.

Objective 04

Purchase Conversion

Place consistent "Buy now" / "Pesan Sekarang" prompts alongside familiar Indonesian payment logos to reduce friction toward ordering.

04 · Marketing Strategy

Four-Pillar Digital Marketing Approach

01 · Brand Positioning

Crispy Tempeh & Sago Heritage

Positions Lariso as an authentic Indonesian tempeh-based snack since 1996—combining savory flavor and durable crunch without synthetic preservatives.

02 · Product Marketing

Ingredients, Benefits & 5 Variants

Highlights core benefits (Renyah Gurih, Tempe Asli, Sagu Alami, Tanpa Pengawet) and showcases 5 distinct 50g flavors: Original, Balado Pedas, Keju Gurih, Telur Asin, and Jagung Bakar.

03 · Social Proof

Customer Testimonials & Trust

Integrates a 5-star review summary ("3158 Reviews" in the showcase prototype) and 5 verified-style testimonial cards reinforcing crispness and daily snack appeal.

04 · Conversion Strategy

Repeated CTAs & Payment Familiarity

Places "Buy now" CTAs in the hero, product showcase, flavor slider, and footer, paired with DANA, GoPay, ShopeePay, OVO, and BCA logos. (Note: Live site is currently a static promotional showcase with placeholder href="#" CTAs rather than a live checkout backend.)

09 · Project Reflection

Connecting Brand Experience & Marketing Communication

This case study demonstrates how digital brand experience and marketing communication reinforce each other: strong packaging visuals create immediate recognition, while structured benefit explanations, product comparisons, and social proof turn curiosity into purchase intent.

At the same time, inspecting the live promotional prototype highlights an important next step for real-world deployment—replacing placeholder template copy/links with verified checkout channels (such as WhatsApp Business or marketplace links) so the accessible purchasing journey ends in a frictionless transaction.
