From Scroll Storytelling to Complete F&B Brand Experience

Lemon Bread — Bakery & More is a conceptual F&B brand positioned as a Transit Refreshment Stop with the tagline "Your Affordable Stop Before You Go."

This portfolio case study documents how an academic-style business plan evolved into an interactive digital brand experience while keeping strategic planning and unvalidated assumptions clearly separated.

    Section F · Honest Summary

    Outcome & Project Status

    A clear boundary between completed digital deliverables and real-world commercial steps still awaiting validation.

    Demonstrated Deliverables

    What Exists Today

    • Verified visual identity system (Fredoka, Nunito, 12-token palette, penguin mascot).
    • Live customer-facing pre-launch website (lemon-scroll-story.vercel.app) with 7 chapters & 4 drink visuals.
    • Live /strategy portal with 2027–2028 roadmap, Gantt timeline, budget allocation, and SWOT flip cards.
    • Documented UX, information architecture, and local Business Analysis companion.
    Pending Real-World Validation

    Not Yet Validated

    • Physical transit booth location survey & finalized operating hours.
    • Final product recipes for Quick Bread & Road Snacks series.
    • Verified HPP calculations, final selling prices, and commercial sales performance.
    • Legal entity verification (PT Lemon Bread Indonesia) and live customer feedback.

    Project Reflection

    The project demonstrates the development of a business concept into a structured digital brand experience, combining strategic thinking, information architecture, visual identity, and frontend implementation—while maintaining strict honesty about what remains conceptual versus verified.
